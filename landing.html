PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

Five modules, one intelligent network

From rainfall data to actionable emergency decisions — everything connected in real-time.

🌧

Rainfall → Runoff

Real-time + IMD forecast rainfall data is instantly transformed into urban runoff predictions using ML hydrology models.

🚰

Drainage Digital Twin

A live 3D digital replica of the entire municipal drainage network — pipes, junctions, channels — monitoring load and overflow risk.

📍

Street-Level Prediction

0–3 hour AI flood forecasts down to individual streets. Know water depth and hazard tier before it happens.

🛡

Emergency Safe Routing

Dynamic flood-aware navigation that continuously reroutes citizens and first responders around hazardous roads.

⚡

What-If Simulation

"What if a critical drain is blocked?" Simulate infrastructure failures and instantly see cascading flood impact.

📡

Citizen Incident Feed

Crowdsourced flood reports from citizens on the ground, merged with sensor data for a unified situational picture.
